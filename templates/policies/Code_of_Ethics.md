# Code of Ethics (Template)

Purpose: Establish standards of conduct, fiduciary duty, and ethical principles.

## Scope
All employees, contractors, officers, and directors.

## Core Principles
- Fiduciary duty and client-first obligations
- Conflicts of interest disclosures and management
- Confidentiality and protection of MNPI/PII
- Personal trading restrictions and PST process

## Reporting and Enforcement
- Reporting channels (CCO contact, anonymous hotline)
- Investigation procedures and sanctions
- Annual certifications
