# SOP: AML Investigation (Template)

## Trigger
- Alert from screening/monitoring or staff report

## Steps
1. Assign case owner and severity
2. Collect KYC files, transactions, counterparties
3. Analyze patterns and risk factors
4. Decide: close, monitor, escalate to SAR
5. Document findings and evidence

## SLAs
- Triage within 1 business day
- Decision within 5 business days where feasible
