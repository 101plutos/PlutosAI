# SOP: Daily Reconciliation (Template)

## Scope
- Positions, cash, and transactions across custodians/admins vs IBOR

## Steps
1. Ingest statements/feeds and normalize
2. Run position, cash, and trade recs
3. Investigate breaks above thresholds
4. Resolve or escalate; document evidence

## SLAs
- Daily completion by 11:00 local
- Escalation on material breaks
