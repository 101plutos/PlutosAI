# Playbook: Security Incident Response (Template)

## Roles
- Incident Commander, Comms Lead, Legal, CISO/CTO, Forensics

## Phases
1. Detect and Triage
2. Contain (short-term and long-term)
3. Eradicate and Recover
4. Post-incident review and corrective actions

## Communications
- Internal status cadence
- Client and regulator notifications where required
