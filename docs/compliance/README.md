# Compliance and Risk Baseline

This section provides a regulator-ready baseline tailored to RIAs/asset managers. Adapt per jurisdiction (SEC, FCA, MAS, ESMA).

## Core Components
- Code of Ethics and P&P Manual
- AML/KYC and sanctions screening
- Conflicts of interest and trade surveillance
- Best execution and soft dollars
- Valuation and pricing governance
- Marketing/advertising review workflows
- Personal trading, gifts/entertainment, OBAs
- BCP/DR, incident response, evidence collection

## Registers and Logs
- Gifts/Entertainment Register
- Personal Trading (PST) Register
- Outside Business Activities Register
- Conflicts Register
- Marketing Materials Log

## Control Mapping
- Map controls to SOC2, ISO27001, NIST CSF, and jurisdictional regs
- Define owners, frequency, evidence, and automation opportunities

## Templates
- Policy templates live under `templates/policies/`
- SOPs and checklists under `templates/sops/` and `templates/checklists/`
