# Investment Operations

Design for accuracy, timeliness, and auditability across middle/back office.

## Functions
- Reconciliation (custodian, fund admin, internal books)
- Cash management and settlements
- Fees and billing
- Corporate actions and income processing
- Performance measurement and GIPS alignment

## Controls
- Daily recs with break thresholds and escalation
- Dual-control on cash movements
- Evidence capture in ticketing system
